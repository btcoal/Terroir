# UF-010A — Run a bounded real-data acquisition spike

[Back to execution backlog](../execution_tickets.md)

**Priority / size:** P0 / S

**Depends on:** UF-010

**Outcome:** The volume, compute, and cost assumptions recorded in UF-005 are
replaced by measurements taken from real filings before M1 commits to a schema,
storage layout, and parser-input closure.

**Why this exists:** UF-005 asks the team to record expected data volume,
compute envelope, service-level objective, and cost assumptions, but the first
ticket that touches real SEC data at scale is UF-023 — roughly fifty
engineering days later on the critical path. Without this spike, every M1
sizing decision rests on estimates that nothing has checked.

**Scope:** 50 to 100 accessions, hand-selected for spread rather than
convenience: early and recent taxonomy vintages, 10-K and 10-Q, at least one
amendment, at least one large accelerated filer with a heavy exhibit list, and
at least one small filer with a sparse instance.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- The spike walks the happy path only: discover the accession, fetch its
  parser-input closure, store it locally, and parse it once with Arelle. Error
  handling, checkpointing, retries, idempotency, and manifest integrity are
  explicitly out of scope and belong to UF-020, UF-012, and UF-023.
- Requests carry a declared user agent and an administrative contact and stay
  under the SEC aggregate rate cap. The spike may prototype this inline, but
  the prototype is throwaway; UF-020 remains the production transport.
- Measurements are recorded per accession and in aggregate: object count and
  total bytes of the parser-input closure, wall time and peak RSS for one
  Arelle parse, taxonomy resolution count and cache-miss rate, and raw fact
  count.
- The report extrapolates storage, compute hours, and egress or object-store
  cost to the full Release 1 universe using the UF-001 eligibility policy, and
  states the assumptions and error bars behind the extrapolation.
- The measured figures are written back into the UF-005 architecture record,
  which is marked as revised rather than edited silently; any ADR whose
  reasoning changes is amended with a pointer to this spike.
- Sizes for UF-011 through UF-016 and UF-020 through UF-027 are reviewed
  against the measurements, and any ticket the evidence shows to be mis-sized
  is resized before work begins on it.
- No spike artifact is promoted: downloaded bytes are discarded or re-acquired
  through UF-023, and no spike code reaches a production path except as a
  reference implementation. The ticket produces a report, not a component.

**Definition of done:** The report is committed and UF-005 records the revised
assumptions. This ticket ships no releasable artifact and is not a Release 1
gate.
