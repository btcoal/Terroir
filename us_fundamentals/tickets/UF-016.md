# UF-016 — Benchmark and select Parquet/DuckDB physical layouts

[Back to execution backlog](../execution_tickets.md)

**Priority / size:** P0 / M

**Depends on:** UF-011, UF-014, UF-021

**Outcome:** Physical layout is selected from representative PIT and
cross-sectional workloads rather than intuition.

**Ordering note:** This ticket is specified in M1 but cannot complete until
UF-021 exists. Synthetic benchmark rows generated without the real accession
inventory understate entity count, per-year row skew, and amendment density,
which are exactly the properties that decide partitioning and row-group
sizing. The ticket is specified in M1 and executed in M2; M1 does not block on
it.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- Candidate long-table layouts partition by available or filing year and
  optionally metric group.
- Benchmarks compare sorting with `metric_id, entity_id, available_at,
  fiscal_period_end` against a company-history projection.
- The test includes an effective-interval table with `valid_from` and
  `valid_to`, plus representative `as_of` queries.
- The synthetic row generator derives its cardinality from the UF-021
  accession inventory: entity count, accessions per entity, filings per year,
  amendment rate, and fiscal-calendar mix are read from the inventory rather
  than assumed, and the generator records the inventory version it used.
- Results report scanned rows/bytes, wall time, storage size, and incremental
  write cost on realistic cardinality.
- The selected layout and row-group sizing are recorded in an ADR with
  reproducible benchmark code.
